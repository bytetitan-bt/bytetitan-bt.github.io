Privacy Policy for Personal Fitness Tracker

With the owner's consent, Personal Fitness Tracker reads activity data from Google Health, including steps, distance, calories and activity minutes. Access is read-only.

Data is stored on a private system and used for personal activity summaries, displays and reminders.

Access to Google Health can be revoked at any time through the owner's Google Account settings. The owner manages and deletes stored data.

Use of Google Health data is subject to the Google Health API Developer and User Data Policy , including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact: personal-fitness-tracker-contact@googlegroups.com
